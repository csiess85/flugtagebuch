First Solo

🇦🇹 LOAV · Bad Vöslau — local flight
★ First Solosolo flightAquila A211OE-AKW▶ Video

Block off 17:32. Role: PIC. Crew: Siess. No second name.

The first solo flight. Three landings, 13 minutes in the air, 1,715 ft at most. On paper the least spectacular entry in weeks — and the one you never forget as long as you live.

Without the second seat occupied the Aquila climbs noticeably better. That is the moment you notice how much an instructor actually weighs, and at the same time how empty the right-hand seat can be.

Eighteen days after Progress Check B, 27 flights and 111 days after the introductory flight. Block on 17:55. After this, everything is different.
