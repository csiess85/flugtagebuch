Across the Vienna Woods to Krems

🇦🇹 LOAV → LOAG · Bad Vöslau → Krems-Langenlois
with instructorAquila A211OE-AKWSiess / P.

The first proper navigation flight: Vöslau — Krems-Langenlois, 72 km as the crow flies, across the Vienna Woods and the Danube near Tulln. Saturday, 08:57, two landings.

LOAG lies at the northern edge of the Wachau, between vineyards and the Kamp valley. The approach from the south leads across the Danube — you see the Wachau from above, Göttweig Abbey on its hill, and then the runway appears between the fields.

The way there is the real exercise: hold the heading, match what you see against the chart, sort out the radio, and take care in good time not to get too close to the Vienna control zone.

Out to the Spitzerberg

🇦🇹 LOAV → LOAS · Bad Vöslau → Spitzerberg
with instructorAquila A211OE-AKWSiess / P.

East this time: across Lake Neusiedl and the Leitha mountains out to the Spitzerberg, the gliding site above the Danube near Hainburg. 98 km, 30 minutes, 106 knots average.

The Spitzerberg is one of the finest airfields in eastern Austria: a grass strip on a ridge of the Hundsheim hills, looking out over the Danube, Devín castle and, on good days, as far as Bratislava. Everything around it is flat — the hill is the only rise for miles, and that makes the approach something special.

Home past Vienna

🇦🇹 LOAS → LOAV · Spitzerberg → Bad Vöslau
with instructorAquila A211OE-AKWSiess / P.

The way back does not follow the same route but runs to the north: along the Danube, past Vienna, across the Vienna Woods and south. 121 km, 42 minutes.

This route is navigation training in its purest form, because it runs along the edge of Vienna's airspace. You do not simply fly straight ahead here — you fly a corridor, with an eye on the clock, the chart and the radio.

Back across the Vienna Woods

🇦🇹 LOAU → LOAV · Stockerau → Bad Vöslau
with instructorAquila A211D-ELFHSiess / P.

97 km, 33 minutes, 3,583 ft, 95 knots average. The way home leads across the Vienna Woods again.

For flying out of Vöslau the Vienna Woods are something like a front door: wooded ridges, hardly any fields to land in, often rough in summer, and behind them the other world begins. You cross them at least twice on every flight to the north.

Across the Bucklige Welt to Graz

🇦🇹 LOAV → LOWG · Bad Vöslau → Graz
with instructorAquila A211OE-AKWSiess / P.

The longest flight so far: 124 km to Graz, straight across the Bucklige Welt and the Styrian hill country. 161 km of ground, 50 minutes, 5,352 ft — a new altitude record since April.

Graz is a controlled commercial airport. That means clearances, transponder, instructions, fitting into scheduled traffic, taxiing at a large airport. For a student pilot that is a step up in kind — flying is suddenly no longer just a matter between you and the landscape.

The route across the Bucklige Welt and the Wechsel is also Austria's classic southern connection: hilly, green, dotted with small villages, and you have to keep your altitude, because the terrain rises slowly beneath you.

with instructorAquila A211OE-AKWSiess / P.

202 km in 64 minutes — the longest distance so far. Back across the hill country, the Bucklige Welt and Wiener Neustadt.

That the return leg is 40 km longer than the outbound one says something: this was not the direct line but an arc. Maybe weather, maybe airspace, maybe simply the wish for a nicer route.
