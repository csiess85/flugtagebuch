Alone to Lake Neusiedl

🇦🇹 LOAV · Bad Vöslau — local flight
solo flightAquila A211OE-AKW

44 minutes solo, out across the Leitha mountains to Lake Neusiedl and back. The first solo flight that leaves the circuit.

This is a transition that looks small in the logbook and is in fact large. In the circuit the airfield is always there. Outside it, finding it again is your own responsibility.

Lake Neusiedl is ideal for this step: 40 km from Vöslau, unmistakable, flat, and on the way back you can navigate by the chain of the Alps, standing in the west like a wall.

Solo to the Spitzerberg

🇦🇹 LOAV → LOAS · Bad Vöslau → Spitzerberg
solo flightAquila A211D-ELFH

37 minutes alone, from Vöslau across Lake Neusiedl and the Leitha mountains to the Spitzerberg. The first solo flight to an airfield away from home.

There is hardly anything that changes your own flying like the first solo landing at an airfield you do not know. Nobody is there to make the position call, nobody to say "the runway is over there". You do it, or you do not.

The Spitzerberg with its grass strip above the Danube is a fine place for it — demanding enough to mean something, friendly enough to work.

Solo to Wels — 171 kilometres on my own

🇦🇹 LOAV → LOLW · Bad Vöslau → Wels
solo flightAquila A211OE-AKW▶ Video

And then: 201 km in 60 minutes, alone, to Wels. The furthest point lies 171 km from the departure airfield.

This is the big solo cross-country. Across the Vienna Woods, then west right through Lower Austria, past St. Pölten and along the Danube into Upper Austria. An hour in the air, alone, with a chart, a radio and a plan.

109 knots average means: it went well. No searching, no detour, nothing taken back. Simply there.

Wels — Krems: the Danube as a guide

🇦🇹 LOLW → LOAG · Wels → Krems-Langenlois
solo flightAquila A211OE-AKW

Second leg of the triangle: 160 km from Wels to Krems-Langenlois, 52 minutes, alone. Along the Danube, through the Wachau, into the Kamp valley.

A triangular flight with two intermediate landings is the classic final piece of navigation training. Three airfields, three approaches, three departures, orienting yourself afresh each time, planning afresh each time — and nobody to take over.

73 minutes alone around Vienna

🇦🇹 LOAV · Bad Vöslau — local flight
solo flightAquila A211OE-AKI▶ Video

The longest solo flight so far: 73 minutes, a big circuit across the Vienna Woods, the Marchfeld, Lake Neusiedl and back over the Leitha mountains.

In early November the air in eastern Austria often has a clarity you never get in summer. No haze, no thermals, sharp shadows. On days like that you can see from above the Vienna Woods as far as the Little Carpathians in Slovakia, and south to the Schneeberg.

Progress Check D

🇦🇹 LOAV · Bad Vöslau — local flight
★ Progress Check Dwith instructorAquila A211D-ELFHSiess / W.PC/D

This flight carries a remark in the logbook that appears nowhere else: PC/D. And a new name in the crew field — W.

94 minutes block, 238 km, three landings, 5,046 ft. A large circuit across the Vienna Woods and St. Pölten, with everything that belongs to the last progress check: navigation, procedures, landings, emergency drills.

It is the second-longest training flight in the whole book. You can tell from the structure — long distance, several landings, a wide altitude band — that this was an examination, not practice.

The fourth and final check before the test. Sixteen days later the day comes.
