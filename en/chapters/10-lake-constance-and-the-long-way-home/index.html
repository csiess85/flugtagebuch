A circuit over Lake Constance

🇦🇹 LOIH · Hohenems-Dornbirn — local flight
solo flightAquila A211D-ELFH

A flight somewhere else entirely: departure and destination is Hohenems-Dornbirn in Vorarlberg, 500 km from home. 48 km, 21 minutes, 5,681 ft.

A short circuit plus an excursion over the Rhine delta and Lake Constance. The Rhine comes out of the Alps here and pushes a grey plume of sediment into the blue lake; from 5,700 ft you see Austria, Switzerland and Germany at the same time, and far to the west Lake Constance running to the horizon.

21 minutes for one of the most spectacular views in central Europe. A good deal.

The long way home: 550 kilometres across Austria

🇦🇹 LOIH → LOAV · Hohenems-Dornbirn → Bad Vöslau
solo flightAquila A211D-ELFH

The closing flight of this logbook, and in every respect the biggest: 550 km, 163 minutes, 7,864 ft, alone, from Hohenems-Dornbirn right across the entire republic to Bad Vöslau.

The list of landmarks reads like a travel brochure: Lake Constance, the Allgäu Alps, the Chiemsee, the Salzkammergut, the Traunsee, the Danube valley, Melk, St. Pölten, the Vienna Woods. Four provinces, two countries, one afternoon.

Two and a half hours alone in an Aquila A211, at 109 knots average with a peak of 164 knots over the ground. Departure at 14:03, block on at 16:55.

It is exactly the kind of flight that would have been unimaginable at the start of this logbook. The first entry, in March 2025, went 16 km and lasted 28 minutes. This one goes 500 km and takes almost three times as long as that whole afternoon did.

A new type at the end

🇦🇹 LOAV · Bad Vöslau — local flight
with instructorPiper PA28 ArcherOE-KBSSiess / T.

One more new thing: OE-KBS, a Piper PA-28 Archer. The third type, with a new name on the right — T.

181 km, 67 minutes, six landings, 4,878 ft, out over the Hohe Wand and back. Converting to a new type always starts the same way: circuits, landings, getting a feel for it.

The PA-28 is again a different animal from the Aquila and the Cessna: a low-wing machine made of sheet metal, broad, good-natured, with a wing that really wants to keep flying just above the ground. Six landings are a good start.

And with that the logbook stands at 92 flights, 215 landings, 77 hours and around 12,300 kilometres — in 17 months. The next entry comes five days later.
