A Morning to Zell am See

August 2026 · 2 flights

654 kilometres out and back in a single morning — through the mountains and along them.

93
Tuesday, 4 August 2026 · 07:37

Across the Alps in the PA-28

🇦🇹 LOAV → LOWZ · Bad Vöslau → Zell am See
solo flightPiper PA28 ArcherOE-KBS

07:37 block off, five days after the first hour on the type. 316 km, 92 minutes, 9,039 ft — across the Alps to Zell am See in the PA-28.

For long stretches the route is the same one as in March: the Hohe Wand, Mariazell, the Erzberg, the Gesäuse, the Ennstal, the Grimming. After that further north than back then — over the Hallstätter See and the Gosaukamm, then along the Hochkönig and past Saalfelden into the Zell valley.

Twenty-five of the 92 minutes are spent above 8,000 ft, at 111 knots average and 167 knots peak over ground. The comparison with March sits right beside it: 303 km in 93 minutes at 106 knots in the Aquila back then, 316 km in 92 minutes at 111 today — more engine, more mass, a little more speed.

In March the same destination was a deep-winter flight, with the Kitzsteinhorn and its full glacier to the south. This time it is a summer morning; the runway at 2,470 ft lies between the lake and the Schmittenhöhe just as it always does. Block on at 09:18 — and the flight home is still to come the same morning.

The way back, north of the mountains

🇦🇹 LOWZ → LOAV · Zell am See → Bad Vöslau
solo flightPiper PA28 ArcherOE-KBS

10:53 block off, 95 minutes after landing. Back to Bad Vöslau, but not the same way: 338 km, 98 minutes, 9,292 ft — past the mountains to the north instead of straight through them.

Out over Kaprun, then northeast: the Gosaukamm, the Wolfgangsee, the Attersee, the Traunsee. From Gmunden on it is the Alpine foreland under the wing — Steyr, the Danube at Ybbs, St. Pölten — then the Vienna Woods and home over Baden.

9,292 ft is the highest point of the day, 26 minutes of it above 8,000 ft, 112 knots average over 98 minutes. Block on at 12:37.

The flight out goes through the mountains, the flight back runs alongside them: first the valleys and the ridges, then the chain of lakes and the plain. 654 km in a single morning, alone, on a type that was still new five days ago.
