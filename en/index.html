From the traffic pattern to the sea

From the introductory flight through four progress checks, the first solo and the skill test all the way to the night rating — eighteen months of VFR flying over eastern Austria, the Alps and four neighbouring countries, recorded by GPS and logbook and retold here flight by flight.

Chapter I

First Circuits

March – May 2025 · 8 flights

From the introductory flight to Progress Check A. Eight flights spent working out which way is up.
Chapter II

The Traffic Pattern

May – June 2025 · 18 flights

Six weeks in which almost every track looks the same: a rectangle over Bad Vöslau, again and again. In between, clearance to taxi solo and Progress Check B.
Chapter III

First Solo

25 June 2025 · 2 flights

Two entries on a Wednesday evening. In the second one, the crew field holds a single name.
Chapter IV

Out of the Pattern

June – July 2025 · 15 flights

Navigation. Unfamiliar airfields, controlled airspace, real routes — and in between, the first longer solo flights.
Chapter V

Alps, Danube and Progress Check C

August 2025 · 5 flights

The biggest day of the training: across the Limestone Alps into the Enns valley, on to Wels at 10,399 feet, and back down the Danube towards home. Three days later, the third check.
Chapter VI

Solo Cross-Country

September – November 2025 · 14 flights

First solo flight beyond the pattern, first solo landing away from home, a triangle across half of Austria — and at the end Progress Check D.
Chapter VII

The Skill Test

November 2025 – January 2026 · 5 flights

The practical test on 27 November. After it: the first flight nobody has to approve any more, and the first one abroad.
Chapter VIII

Night VFR

January – February 2026 · 10 flights

Six evenings between dusk and darkness, 23 landings in the black — and on 18 February the first solo flight at night.
Chapter IX

Across the Borders

March – June 2026 · 12 flights

Zell am See, Bavaria, Czechia, the Adriatic. Four countries in four months, every one of them flown solo.
Chapter X

A Summer over Austria

July – August 2026 · 6 flights

Straight home from Lake Constance, a new type, a morning out to Zell am See and back, and a solar eclipse over Graz at the end of it.
